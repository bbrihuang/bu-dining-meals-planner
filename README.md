# Dining Hall Planner

Tell it how many calories and how much protein, carbs and fat you want in a day, and it
builds a plate for every meal from what your dining hall is serving, with exact amounts
like "1½ cups" or "3 links".

**Schools:** Boston University (Warren Towers, Marciano Commons, West Campus and Fenway).
More are on the way.

## Getting started

1. **Pick your school**, then **pick a dining hall**. Each hall's card shows today's meal
   hours.
2. **Pick a day.** Menus are posted up to two weeks ahead.
3. **Set your daily targets**: calories, protein, carbs and fat. Type them in, or use
   **Calculate my macros** (see below).
4. **Read your plan.** You get one plate per meal, and together they add up to your day.

## Your daily targets

Type a number into any of the four boxes and the plan updates as you type. A number turns
red if it's out of range (calories must be between 1,000 and 6,000).

The bar under the boxes shows how your calories split between protein, carbs and fat. If
your macros add up to a lot more or less than your calorie target, you'll see a warning.
Protein and carbs are 4 calories per gram, and fat is 9.

Your targets are saved in your browser, so they'll be there next time.

On a phone, the targets fold into one line at the top. Tap it to edit them.

### Calculate my macros

Not sure what your targets should be? Click **Calculate my macros** and answer four
quick sections:

1. **Body:** age, height, weight, and optionally your goal weight and body fat %.
   Switch between lb/ft and kg/cm whenever you like, and your numbers convert.
2. **Activity:** your usual daily steps and how many workouts you do a week.
3. **Goal:** Fast cut (−500 cal), Slow cut (−250), Maintain, Lean bulk (+250) or
   Bulk (+500).
4. **Macro balance:** how much protein you want (grams per pound of body weight, or of
   goal weight if you entered one), and whether the rest leans toward carbs or fat.

Your targets update as you go. Click **Use these targets** to fill them in. The
calculator remembers your answers.

This is an estimate for healthy adults, not medical advice.

## Your meal plan

The top of the plan shows your **day total** against your targets. Below that is a card
for each meal (breakfast, lunch, dinner and so on), with its hours and its plate.

Each line on a plate shows:

- **how much to take**, in amounts you can actually serve yourself, such as whole
  pieces, quarter cups or whole ounces
- the dish and the station it's at
- its calories, protein, carbs and fat

Two buttons let you change the plan:

- **Another option** shows a different plate for that meal. The counter (for example
  2/5) shows how many plates fit.
- **✕ (Not this)** next to a dish rules it out, and the whole day is re-planned without
  it. Use it for dishes you don't like, can't eat, or the hall has run out of. Click
  **Bring back** at the top to undo it.

Each meal aims at what's left of your day after the meals before it. If you swap
breakfast, lunch and dinner adjust to make up the difference.

## Full menu

Switch the view from **Meal plan** to **Full menu** to see everything the hall serves at
each meal, grouped by station.

- The menu starts on the meal being served now, or the next one today.
- **Search this menu** finds dishes or stations by name.
- Tap a dish for its description, fiber, sugar, sodium and allergens.
- **Create Your Own** stations (marked +) open to show every option you can build with.
- Tags: **VG** vegan, **V** vegetarian, **H** halal, **GF** gluten free.

## Good to know

- **Switch halls** by clicking the hall's name at the top of the page. Your day and
  targets stay the same.
- **Share a day:** the address in your browser includes the date and view, so you can
  send someone the exact menu you're looking at.
- **Menus refresh twice a day**, at about 5 am and 2 pm. The time of the last update is at
  the bottom of the page. **Official menu** links to the hall's own page.
- **Dark mode** is the switch in the bar at the top of the page.
- Nutrition numbers come from the dining halls' own menus. Allergen info is only as
  complete as theirs. If you have a serious allergy, check with the staff.
